inline text formatting




  • strong

    • Gives bold styling and implies strong importance.

  • b

    • Renders bold text solely for visual presentation.

  • em

    • Applies italic styling to denote semantic emphasis.

  • i

    • Renders italic text solely for visual presentation.

  • u

    • Adds a non-textual underline annotation to content.

  • small

    • Decrements text sizes for notices and side-comments.

  • s

    • Renders text with a strikethrough line to mark inaccuracy.

  • del

    • Marks block or text components removed from a document.

  • ins

    • Highlights content newly inserted into the file.

  • sub

    • Formats text lower on the line as a subscript.

  • sup

    • Formats text higher on the line as a superscript.

  • mark

    • Highlights background text to grab reader focus.

  • abbr

    • Defines acronyms and abbreviations with hover titles.

  • cite

    • Credits the original title of a cited piece of work.

  • q

    • Wraps inline text segments in quotation marks.

  • code

    • Styles a short snippet of executable code.

  • kbd

    • Designates physical user keyboard inputs.

  • samp

    • Denotes terminal or program output samples.

  • var

    • Displays mathematical or programmatic variables.

  • dfn

    • Signals the defining instance of a specific term.

  • data

    • Links explicit text strings to machine-readable equivalents.

  • time

    • Translates dates or times into machine-readable structures.

  • bdo

    • Enforces bi-directional text presentation directions.

  • bdi

    • Isolates text directions from surrounding language.
